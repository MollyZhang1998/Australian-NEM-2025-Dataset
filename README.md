# Australian NEM 2025 Dataset for MSGDG-STN

This repository contains the full-year 2025 electricity price and demand data used in the experiments of our paper:

“Multi-Scale Gated Dual-Graph Spatio-Temporal Network for Multi-Region Electricity Price Forecasting in Australian Market”

## Dataset

The dataset covers the entire year of 2025 at a 5-minute temporal resolution for the five regions of the Australian National Electricity Market (NEM):

- NSW
- QLD
- VIC
- SA
- TAS

The data are organized by region, with 12 monthly CSV files for each region.

## Data source

The original data are publicly available from the Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO):

https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem

## Repository structure

data/

- VIC/
